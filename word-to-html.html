Scribe: Word to HTML v1.0

Paste a whole Word document. Clean prose + editable tables in one pass.

Input
Paste is preferred (more faithful). .docx is a secondary path.
Please paste or provide some content before cleaning.
Applied top-to-bottom. Default: FedDev Ontario → FedDev Ontario, $N million → $N million.
  1. Paste from Word into the box on the left (or upload a .docx, or paste raw HTML).
  2. Click Clean. Prose is auto-cleaned; tables become editable.
  3. Click any table cell to activate it. The editing toolbar appears above the table — drag to select cells, then use the buttons (Header, Merge, Bold, Indent, etc.).
  4. Fill in caption (Table #, Title, Unit) at the top of the toolbar if needed.
  5. Click Copy HTML at the bottom (or in the navbar) and paste into your Canada.ca page.

Tip: Prose is read-only — fix typos in Word and re-paste, or hand-edit the raw HTML in the output panel (changes there do not sync back to the canvas). Use the Advanced panel to tune which attributes/tags are stripped, or to toggle Smart Replacements like FedDev Ontario.
